Your way in

The road to 818

You do not have to wait for the event to start. Most of the work happens on our Discord, before you move.

I

Server Transfer, phase one

Each kingdom's Transfer Manager sets a power cap. Governors under the cap can move freely in the open phase. Talk to us now.

II

Invite phase

Governors above the cap need an invite from a Transfer Manager. Tell us your power ahead of time so we can plan invites.

III

Open transfer

The last two days, Friday and Saturday (UTC). Free slots go first come, first served, so be online when it opens.

Before you move

Leave your current alliance. You cannot be a King or Transfer Manager, and you cannot be in an active battle.

Transfer Passes

The number depends on your Transfer Score compared with the target kingdom. Collect them before the event starts.

Cooldown

One transfer per event, with a cooldown of about 30 days after your last move. The in-game rules always win if something changes.
